ANKA'S LAST LETTER
Story Dialogue – Chapter 1

==================================================
CHAPTER 1 – THE LETTER
==================================================

SCENE 1 – ANKA'S HOUSE
==================================================

[Mother]
Anka, you're awake. I thought you'd sleep a little longer.

[Anka]
I couldn't. Something has been bothering me since yesterday.

[Mother]
Your father used to say the same thing when he was your age.

[Anka]
Mom... why did you mention him?

[Mother]
Because someone came to the village this morning.

[Anka]
Who?

[Mother]
A traveler. He was looking for you.

[Anka]
Looking for me?

[Mother]
He left this for you.

[Anka]
A letter?

[Mother]
Yes. I haven't opened it. It has your name on it.

[Anka]
The handwriting...

[Mother]
You recognize it?

[Anka]
It's Dad's.

[Mother]
Anka...

[Anka]
He wrote this?

[Mother]
I don't know. Open it.

[Anka]
(reading)
"If this letter reaches you, come to Greyford. There are things you deserve to know."

[Mother]
Greyford...

[Anka]
You know the place?

[Mother]
I know enough to tell you not to go there alone.

[Anka]
Then you know something about Dad.

[Mother]
There are things your father never told me either.

[Anka]
I'm going to find him.

[Mother]
Anka, we don't even know if he's still there.

[Anka]
Then I'll find out what happened.

[Mother]
At least speak with the Elder before you leave.

[Anka]
I will.


==================================================
SCENE 2 – VILLAGE SQUARE
==================================================

[Anka]
The whole village feels different today.

[Mira]
Anka! I've been looking everywhere for you.

[Anka]
Mira? What's wrong?

[Mira]
Thomas said a traveler arrived this morning asking about your family.

[Anka]
He left a letter for me.

[Mira]
Your father's letter?

[Anka]
Yes.

[Mira]
Then you're really going to look for him?

[Anka]
I have to.

[Mira]
You shouldn't go alone.

[Anka]
I'm not planning to disappear. I'll come back.

[Mira]
Promise?

[Anka]
I promise.


==================================================
SCENE 3 – ELDER'S HOUSE
==================================================

[Elder]
Come in, Anka. Your mother told me you received a letter.

[Anka]
She told you?

[Elder]
She was worried.

[Anka]
Do you know anything about Greyford?

[Elder]
Your father spent several years there.

[Anka]
Why didn't anyone tell me?

[Elder]
Because your father wanted to leave the past behind.

[Anka]
What happened?

[Elder]
He was investigating a company called Northbridge Works.

[Anka]
A company?

[Elder]
They were buying land around the old railway town.

[Anka]
And Dad got involved?

[Elder]
He believed some of the villagers were being treated unfairly.

[Anka]
Is that why he disappeared?

[Elder]
I don't know.

[Anka]
Then I'll find out.

[Elder]
The road to Greyford is blocked. The old bridge was damaged last night.

[Anka]
Can it be repaired?

[Elder]
Ask Bjorn. If anyone can repair it, he can.


==================================================
SCENE 4 – GENERAL STORE
==================================================

[Thomas]
Anka! Come in.

[Anka]
Thomas, did you see the traveler who came this morning?

[Thomas]
The man with the brown coat?

[Anka]
Yes.

[Thomas]
He asked about your father.

[Anka]
What did he say?

[Thomas]
He said your father was working in Greyford until recently.

[Anka]
Recently?

[Thomas]
That's what he said.

[Anka]
Did he leave anything else?

[Thomas]
Only this.

[Anka]
A railway ticket...

[Thomas]
Greyford Station. It looks old.

[Anka]
Thanks, Thomas.

[Thomas]
Be careful, Anka. Some questions are easier to ask than to answer.


==================================================
SCENE 5 – FARM
==================================================

[Miller]
Anka! Could you give me a hand?

[Anka]
What's happened?

[Miller]
The storm damaged the water channel. Without it, the fields won't get enough water.

[Anka]
I'll fix it.

[Miller]
Thanks. After that, go see Bjorn. He was checking the bridge earlier.

[Anka]
I'll talk to him.


==================================================
SCENE 6 – BLACKSMITH
==================================================

[Bjorn]
You're looking for me?

[Anka]
The Elder said you were checking the bridge.

[Bjorn]
I was. The main support beam is badly damaged.

[Anka]
Can you repair it?

[Bjorn]
Not alone. I need some iron brackets from the storage shed.

[Anka]
Where is it?

[Bjorn]
Behind the old mill.

[Anka]
I'll get them.

[Bjorn]
And Anka...

[Anka]
Yes?

[Bjorn]
Your father used to come here.

[Anka]
What did he want?

[Bjorn]
He asked questions about the railway.

[Anka]
The railway?

[Bjorn]
That's all I remember.


==================================================
SCENE 7 – OLD MILL / STORAGE SHED
==================================================

[Anka]
These must be the brackets.

[Anka]
Wait... what's this?

[Anka]
Dad's initials.

[Anka]
He was here.

[Anka]
Then the letter wasn't a coincidence.


==================================================
SCENE 8 – BRIDGE
==================================================

[Bjorn]
Good. These should hold.

[Anka]
How long will the repair last?

[Bjorn]
Long enough for you to cross.

[Anka]
Thank you.

[Bjorn]
Where are you heading?

[Anka]
Greyford.

[Bjorn]
Your father went there looking for answers.

[Anka]
I'm going there to find mine.

[Bjorn]
Then take care.


==================================================
SCENE 9 – VILLAGE EXIT
==================================================

[Mira]
You're leaving already?

[Anka]
The bridge is repaired.

[Mira]
When will you come back?

[Anka]
When I know the truth.

[Mira]
Take this.

[Anka]
Your old compass?

[Mira]
You gave it to me when we were children.

[Anka]
You kept it all these years?

[Mira]
Maybe I knew you'd need it someday.

[Anka]
I'll bring it back.

[Mira]
Just come back, Anka.

[Anka]
I will.


==================================================
CHAPTER 1 END
