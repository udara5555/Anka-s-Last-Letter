ANKA'S LAST LETTER
CHAPTER 1 — REVISED STORY + GAMEPLAY SCRIPT
MAP-BASED VERSION

CURRENT PLAYER PROGRESS
=======================

Scene 2 is already completed:
- Anka has spoken with Mira.
- Anka has Father's Letter.
- Anka has Father's old Travel Bag.
- Anka has the Family Photograph.

The chapter now continues from the Village Square.

SCENE 3 — GENERAL STORE
=======================

[Anka]
Thomas, did a traveler come here this morning?

[Thomas]
He did. Brown coat. Didn't buy anything.

[Anka]
What did he want?

[Thomas]
He asked about your father.

[Anka]
What did you tell him?

[Thomas]
That your father used to work near Greyford.

[Anka]
What are you not telling me?

[Thomas]
He asked about the old bridge.

TASK 1 — INVESTIGATE THE STORE
------------------------------

Player can inspect:
- Old railway supplies
- Thomas's delivery records
- Locked storage shelf
- Village map

DISCOVERY:
An old Greyford delivery receipt.

CLUE:
Father -> Greyford -> Village Bridge -> Town

SCENE 4 — ELDER'S HOUSE
=======================

[Elder]
Your father knew that bridge very well.

[Anka]
Why?

[Elder]
He used it whenever he traveled toward Greyford.

[Anka]
Then I'll cross it.

[Elder]
You can't. It's damaged.

[Anka]
Can it be repaired?

[Elder]
Ask Bjorn.

The Elder notices Anka's family photograph.

[Elder]
Where did you get that?

[Anka]
It belonged to Dad.

The Elder becomes uncomfortable.

This tells the player that the photograph may contain important information.

SCENE 5 — FARM
==============

[Miller]
Anka! Before you reach the bridge, I need some help.

[Anka]
What's wrong?

[Miller]
The irrigation gate broke during the storm.

[Anka]
I'll fix it.

TASK 2 — REPAIR THE IRRIGATION SYSTEM
-------------------------------------

Player must:
1. Inspect the water channel.
2. Find the broken wooden pin.
3. Find suitable wood.
4. Use the knife from the travel bag to shape it.
5. Install the pin.
6. Open the water gate.

REWARD:
Strong Wood Piece

The wood will later be used for bridge repair.

SCENE 6 — BLACKSMITH
====================

[Anka]
The Elder said you can repair the bridge.

[Bjorn]
I can repair part of it.

[Anka]
What do you need?

[Bjorn]
Metal brackets and a strong wooden support.

[Anka]
I have the wood.

[Bjorn]
Good. I'll provide the metal.

TASK 3 — CRAFT THE BRIDGE REPAIR KIT
------------------------------------

BJORN PROVIDES:
- Iron Bar
- Leather Strip

PLAYER HAS:
- Strong Wood Piece

CRAFTING RECIPE:
Iron Bar + Strong Wood + Leather Strip = BRIDGE REPAIR KIT

SCENE 7 — WATERFALL INVESTIGATION
=================================

The player notices an abandoned campsite near the Waterfall.

PLAYER CAN INSPECT:
- Broken lantern
- Empty food container
- Brown cloth
- Scrap of paper

DISCOVERY:
The brown cloth resembles the traveler's coat.

Paper says:

"Greyford — don't use the main road."

[Anka]
So the traveler came through here.

TASK 4 — INVESTIGATE THE CAMPSITE
---------------------------------

Player must:
- Compare the cloth with the traveler's description.
- Inspect the paper.
- Search the campsite for another clue.

OPTIONAL CLUE:
A small metal button matching the traveler's coat.

SCENE 8 — OLD BRIDGE
====================

[Bjorn]
Good. Let's see if this holds.

TASK 5 — REPAIR THE OLD BRIDGE
------------------------------

Bridge interaction puzzle:
1. Place wooden support.
2. Attach metal brackets.
3. Tighten leather straps.
4. Push the damaged beam into place.
5. Test the bridge.

After repair, Anka notices a carving:

A.C.

[Anka]
Dad was here.

This is the first major physical proof connecting Anka's father to the village route.

SCENE 9 — INSPECT FATHER'S TRAVEL BAG
=====================================

After discovering his father's initials, Anka checks the travel bag more carefully.

PLAYER CAN INSPECT:
- Main compartment
- Inner pocket
- Hidden bottom compartment
- Old tag

ITEMS DISCOVERED:
- Old Compass
- Railway Work Tag
- Small Brass Key
- Photograph Holder

The photograph holder has a message:

"Ask for the man who remembers Platform Two."

[Anka]
Platform Two...

TASK 6 — CONNECT THE CLUES
--------------------------

Player opens the journal.

Clues:
1. Father's Letter
2. Greyford Delivery Receipt
3. Brown Traveler Cloth
4. Railway Work Tag
5. Father's Initials on Bridge
6. Platform Two message

PLAYER connects:
Father -> Railway
Railway -> Greyford
Greyford -> Platform Two
Traveler -> Old Bridge
Bridge -> Father's presence

SCENE 10 — MIRA'S HOUSE
=======================

[Anka]
Mira, look at this.

Anka shows the family photograph.

[Mira]
I've seen that man before.

[Anka]
Who is he?

[Mira]
I don't know his name.

[Anka]
Then where did you see him?

[Mira]
My father used to talk about him.

[Anka]
Where?

[Mira]
Greyford.

Mira gives Anka a compass.

[Anka]
It doesn't point north.

[Mira]
I know.

[Anka]
Then it's broken.

[Mira]
No. The needle is attracted to iron.

TASK 7 — LEARN TO USE THE COMPASS
---------------------------------

Player tests the compass near:
- Iron gate
- Bridge
- Blacksmith's tools
- Railway debris

The compass reacts most strongly near old railway metal.

It points toward the Forest Path.

SCENE 11 — FOREST PATH
======================

The player follows the compass.

Along the path are old wooden markers.

PLAYER MUST:
1. Follow the compass.
2. Inspect the wooden markers.
3. Avoid dead-end paths.
4. Find an abandoned railway sign.
5. Read the sign.

SIGN:
"GREYFORD — 12 KM"

The road ahead is blocked by fallen trees.

[Anka]
So this is the road Dad used.

[Anka]
Greyford is waiting.

FINAL TASK — PREPARE TO LEAVE
=============================

Player must:
1. Equip Father's Letter.
2. Equip the Compass.
3. Carry the Travel Bag.
4. Carry the Family Photograph.
5. Choose one useful crafted tool.
6. Reach the forest road.
