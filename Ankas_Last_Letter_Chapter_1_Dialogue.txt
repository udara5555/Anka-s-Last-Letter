ANKA'S LAST LETTER
CHAPTER 1 — THE LETTER THAT ARRIVED TOO LATE
FULL DIALOGUE + GAMEPLAY TASK SCRIPT

==================================================
GAMEPLAY DESIGN
==================================================

Chapter goal:
Anka receives a letter that appears to be from his father, who disappeared five years ago.
The letter was written only three months ago and tells Anka to find Greyford's old railway station
and ask about Platform Two.

The player should not simply follow waypoint markers. They should:
- inspect objects
- question NPCs
- compare clues
- craft simple equipment
- solve environmental problems
- remember information from previous conversations

Main gameplay loop:
EXPLORE -> TALK -> DISCOVER CLUE -> SOLVE PROBLEM -> CRAFT / USE ITEM -> TALK AGAIN

==================================================
SCENE 1 — ANKA'S HOUSE
==================================================

[Mother]
Anka, you're awake. I thought you'd sleep longer.

[Anka]
I couldn't sleep properly.

[Mother]
I know.

[Mother]
Someone came to the village this morning.

[Anka]
Who?

[Mother]
I don't know. He only asked whether you still lived here.

[Mother]
He left this for you.

[Anka]
That handwriting... It's Dad's.

[Mother]
You recognize it?

[Anka]
How did you get this?

[Mother]
The traveler said it had to reach you.

==================================================
TASK 1 — INSPECT THE LETTER
==================================================

Player inspects:
- Wax seal
- Handwriting
- Date
- Address

IMPORTANT DISCOVERY:
The letter is dated THREE MONTHS AGO.

[Anka]
He disappeared five years ago...
How did he write this three months ago?

Letter:
"If this letter reaches you, go to Greyford.
Find the old railway station.
Ask about Platform Two."

[Anka]
Platform Two?

[Mother]
There is no Platform Two anymore.

[Anka]
You know about it?

[Mother]
Your father didn't talk about Greyford much.

==================================================
TASK 2 — SEARCH THE HOUSE
==================================================

Player can inspect:
- Father's old travel bag
- Family photograph
- Wooden toolbox
- Mother's cupboard

Discovery:
An old railway work tag:
"GREYFORD RAILWAY SERVICE"

Optional discovery:
A torn railway timetable.

==================================================
SCENE 2 — VILLAGE SQUARE
==================================================

[Mira]
Anka! I've been looking everywhere for you.

[Anka]
Did you hear about the traveler?

[Mira]
Everyone did.

[Anka]
Did you see him?

[Mira]
Only from far away. Thomas spoke to him.

==================================================
TASK 3 — INVESTIGATE THE TRAVELER
==================================================

No quest marker.

Player can:
- Ask Mira
- Ask Thomas
- Inspect village gate
- Talk to villagers
- Inspect market

Clue:
The traveler walked toward the old mill.

==================================================
SCENE 3 — GENERAL STORE
==================================================

[Thomas]
Morning, Anka.

[Anka]
A traveler came here today.

[Thomas]
He did.

[Anka]
What did he want?

[Thomas]
He asked about your father.

[Anka]
What exactly?

[Thomas]
He asked whether your father ever worked at Greyford Station.

[Anka]
Did he leave anything?

[Thomas]
Only this.

ITEM:
Old railway token marked:
"GREYFORD — PLATFORM 2"

==================================================
TASK 4 — CRAFT AN INVESTIGATION KIT
==================================================

Thomas sells:
- Notebook
- Charcoal pencil
- Folding knife
- Rope

Player chooses what to buy with limited money.

Craft:
Notebook + charcoal = JOURNAL
Rope + cloth strip = BASIC ROPE KIT

==================================================
SCENE 4 — OLD MILL
==================================================

Player inspects:
- Door
- Floor
- Machinery
- Wooden beams
- Locked box
- Footprints
- Broken window

==================================================
TASK 5 — FOLLOW THE TRAVELER'S TRAIL
==================================================

Footprints lead:
Door -> Storage room -> Back wall -> Broken window

At window:
A torn piece of brown fabric.

ITEM:
BROWN FABRIC

Father's initials are carved into a beam.

[Anka]
Dad...

==================================================
TASK 6 — GET THE MILL KEY
==================================================

Storage room is locked.

Player needs to investigate who may have a spare key.

Talk to Miller.

==================================================
SCENE 5 — FARM
==================================================

[Miller]
You need the old mill key?

[Anka]
Yes.

[Miller]
It was lost years ago.

[Anka]
Then how did my father get into that room?

[Miller]
He didn't use the front door.

Miller asks Anka to repair the irrigation gate.

==================================================
TASK 7 — REPAIR THE WATER CHANNEL
==================================================

Player must:
1. Find where water stops.
2. Find a missing wooden pin.
3. Cut a replacement with the folding knife.
4. Fit the pin.
5. Turn the wooden gate.
6. Redirect water to the field.

[Miller]
You have your father's patience.

[Anka]
I don't remember him fixing things.

[Miller]
You didn't see everything he did.

Reward:
MILL KEY

==================================================
SCENE 6 — OLD MILL STORAGE ROOM
==================================================

Player unlocks the room.

Objects:
- Old calendar
- Railway timetable
- Wooden ruler
- Metal box

==================================================
TASK 8 — SOLVE THE BOX COMBINATION
==================================================

The box has a three-number lock.

Clues:
Calendar -> circled date
Timetable -> train departure time
Ruler -> three unusual marks

Player connects the clues to determine the combination.

Inside:
- Old photograph
- Railway badge
- Torn map
- Letter fragment

Letter fragment:
"...do not send this through the station.
Someone is watching the railway office."

[Anka]
Dad wasn't just working there.
He was investigating something.

==================================================
SCENE 7 — BLACKSMITH
==================================================

[Bjorn]
You look like you've found trouble.

[Anka]
I found this.

[Bjorn]
Your father had one of those.

[Anka]
What was he doing at the railway?

[Bjorn]
Asking questions.

[Anka]
About what?

[Bjorn]
Land purchases. Railway contracts. Northbridge Works.

==================================================
TASK 9 — CRAFT A REPAIR TOOL
==================================================

The bridge is damaged.

Bjorn needs a:
LEVER TOOL

Required:
- Iron bar -> Bjorn
- Wooden handle -> old mill
- Leather grip -> Miller

Player must communicate with the NPCs and collect components.

Workbench recipe:
Iron bar + wooden handle + leather grip = LEVER TOOL

==================================================
SCENE 8 — OLD BRIDGE
==================================================

[Bjorn]
Good. Put the lever under the beam.

Player positions the tool correctly and repairs the bridge.

A hidden metal maintenance plate becomes accessible.

==================================================
TASK 10 — OPEN THE RAILWAY MAINTENANCE PLATE
==================================================

Use the railway key.

Inside:
- Maintenance records
- Map
- Northbridge Works documents
- Date from five years ago

Marked line:
"PLATFORM TWO — ACCESS CLOSED"

[Anka]
Platform Two existed.
Someone removed it from the records.

Northbridge Works.

==================================================
TASK 11 — CONNECT THE EVIDENCE
==================================================

Evidence:
1. Father's letter
2. Railway badge
3. Railway token
4. Maintenance record
5. Map
6. Elder's information

Player connects the clues:

LETTER -> GREYFORD
BADGE -> FATHER
MAINTENANCE RECORD -> PLATFORM TWO
MAP -> FOREST SERVICE ROAD

Correct arrangement reveals:
FOREST SERVICE ROUTE

==================================================

[Anka]
Yes.

[Anka]
It doesn't point north.

[Anka]

[Mira]
No. The needle is attracted to iron.

==================================================

==================================================

Player tests the compass near:
- Iron gate
- Bridge
- Tool chest
- Railway debris

Strongest signal points toward:
FOREST SERVICE ROAD

The player learns to use environmental clues instead of quest markers.

==================================================

[Anka]

[Mother]

[Anka]
By disappearing?

[Mother]

Mother gives Anka an old photograph.

Take this.

[Anka]

[Mother]

==================================================
FINAL TASK — LEAVE FOR GREYFORD
==================================================

Player must:
1. Equip notebook
2. Equip compass
3. Carry father's letter
4. Choose one crafted tool
5. Enter forest service road
